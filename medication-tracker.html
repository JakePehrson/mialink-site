Features

A record you can trust, on your own terms

Schedules, reminders, inventory and history are designed to work together — so a dose you record updates the day, the stock and the trends at once.

01 Flexible medication schedules

Set up daily medications, selected weekdays, every-X-day schedules or as-needed tracking. Add multiple reminder times, treatment cycles, an end date or a dose-count limit. Pause or complete a medication while keeping its recorded history.

02 A clear view of your day

See scheduled medications and weight reminders together on Today. Record taken or skipped doses, review actual times and amounts, and correct entries when needed. Use the calendar and filtered history to revisit earlier records.

03 Reminders with practical controls

Choose medication and weight reminders independently. Snooze for 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours in the app. Supported Android notifications offer quick actions, including a 10-minute snooze and optional Taken/Skip actions while locked. Choose discreet medication text for more privacy.

04 Injection and injection-pen tracking

Track injection locations, customize site lists and organize medications into shared rotation groups. Review rotation suggestions and recent site use based on your recorded history. Injection pens have their own administration method and icon.

05 Medication inventory

Track containers, remaining stock, expiration dates and stock adjustments. Link inventory to medications and review deductions when recording a dose. Set optional low-stock alerts and keep a record of stock activity.

06 Weight tracking that fits your routine

Record weight in pounds or kilograms and review your history over the time range you choose. Switch between All, AM and PM graph views. On supported Android devices, optionally import weight through Health Connect or share manual entries, with separate controls for each direction.

07 Graphs and side-by-side trends

View recorded medication amounts and weight over time. Compare two medications, a medication and weight, or AM and PM weight trends. Select date ranges, expand graphs and inspect the underlying data tables.

08 Reconstitution and syringe reference tools Android

Use the reconstitution calculator with values you enter and verify. Choose common syringe capacities or save a named custom configuration with its marking spacing. Optionally show a configured syringe illustration during medication review and reuse compatible saved inventory preparation settings after review.

09 Make it your own

Choose light or dark appearance, color palettes, medication colors and icons, custom units and injection-site names. Keep your preferred display settings as you move between screens.

10 Locally controlled records and file backups

Core tracking keeps its records on your device and needs no app account. Export a backup file to keep a separate copy or transfer your records, and restore from a saved backup when needed.

Check the reference, not just the picture

Syringe illustrations are references only. Independently verify the prescribed dose, volume and actual syringe markings before use. Do not rely on an illustration alone.

Reminders depend on your device

Reminders depend on device permissions and settings. Reopen the app regularly to refresh upcoming reminders, and keep a separate backup of your records.
