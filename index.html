About Mialink

A small studio with a long attention span

Mialink Software LLC builds software for people who need to keep a reliable record of something — a medication routine, a blend, a note they will want later. That means fewer features, finished properly: schedules that hold, numbers that add up, and records that stay available.

Our products are made in-house, from the data model to the last empty state. We keep your data under your control, keep the interface out of the way, and keep improving them after release.

How we work

  • 1
    Start with the routine

    We map the real-world process before deciding what the screens should look like.

  • 2
    Build for the record

    Accurate history, clear corrections and exports — not just a pretty dashboard.

  • 3
    Keep it in your hands

    Our products need no account, and keep their records on your device.

  • 4
    Say what a tool does not do

    Limits belong on the product page, not in a footnote. It is the fastest way to earn the right kind of trust.
