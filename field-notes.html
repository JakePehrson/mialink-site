About this release

Field Notes Companion 1.5.0 runs on Chrome and Edge for Windows, macOS and Linux. Store listings are being prepared; until they are live, this page and the privacy policy are the reference for what the extension does and how it handles your notes.

Features

Notes that attach to meaning, not to pixels

Most annotation tools mark a spot on a screen. Field Notes Companion attaches to the thing itself — a field's id, a label's target, a column header's text — and finds it again on every page load.

01 Annotate anything with a label

Field labels, column headers, section legends, plain text. If the page names it, you can pin a note to it — and the marker is re-resolved against the live page, so a note written next to "Renewal date" is still there after the app ships an update.

02 Hover to read, click to edit

A small marker sits beside each annotated element. Hovering opens the note after a delay you set, from half a second to thirty seconds, and long notes open into a wider layout instead of a cramped tooltip.

03 Notes the app never sees

Everything is kept in your browser's extension storage. Nothing is written into the application, so you cannot corrupt a record, break a form, or leave a note where a colleague will read it over your shoulder.

04 Rich notes, not just text

Bold, italic, underline, highlights, text colour, bulleted and numbered lists, links, and note, warning and example callout blocks.

05 Scoped the way you need

By default a note follows the exact page you wrote it on, with long IDs and GUIDs in the address normalised so the same screen still matches. Turn on Ignore URL and match on screen and the note follows you across every account, ticket or order.

06 Make it invisible, or make it loud

Choose the marker shape, its colour, the note background, a size from 8 to 22 pixels and an opacity, and override any of it per note. If you make them too faint to find, one button resets every marker to its defaults.

07 Backups you actually own

Export a dated JSON file, then restore with a merge or a clean replace. A reminder nudges you every 14 days by default, and you can change the interval or silence it.

08 Deletions that stay deleted

Deleting a note records a tombstone with a timestamp. That is what stops a sync bringing back a note you removed on another machine, or an old backup quietly resurrecting everything you have deleted since.

Also in the extension

  • Ctrl+Shift+Y starts a note without touching the toolbar
  • A floating toolbar you can drag out of the way, which remembers where you left it
  • One toggle that hides every marker and note, for screenshots or a shared screen
  • Named note sets for production, staging or a client's environment
  • Import someone else's export into a set of its own, without touching yours

Markers hide themselves

If the target is not on screen — a tab you have not opened, a collapsed section, a field hidden by permissions — the marker is simply not drawn. No orphaned markers floating over empty space.

Notes are only ever read to the extent needed to find the element they are anchored to and draw the marker. There is no model, no summarising and no suggestion engine behind any of this.

Storage

In your browser, or in your own Drive

There is no account with us, because there is no service to sign into. Notes live in your browser's extension storage. If you want two machines in step, the extension can write one file to your own Google Drive — after you turn that on and sign in.

Drive sync uses the drive.file scope, which lets the extension see only the single file it created. It cannot read, list or modify anything else in your Drive.

How sync behaves

  • 1
    Manual sync shows a preview

    How many notes are coming in, how many changes, deletions and conflicts, before anything is written.

  • 2
    Background sync stops at a conflict

    It checks on the interval you set, and when two versions disagree it pauses and asks instead of picking a winner.

  • 3
    Conflicts are reviewed side by side

    One at a time, with your choice of which version wins, and nothing silently resolved.

Honest limits

What it is not

Worth saying plainly, because it decides whether this is the right tool for the way you work.

Desktop Chromium only

Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS and Linux. There is no mobile browser support and no Safari or Firefox build.

Not a collaboration tool

No real-time sharing, no comments and no team workspace. You share notes by exporting a set and sending the file.

It needs something to anchor to

If an app draws its interface into a canvas or hides everything inside a shadow DOM, there may be no field id, label or header for a note to attach to.

Sync is file-based, and notes are not encrypted

Sync moves notes between machines through your Drive; it is not a live connection. Notes are stored unencrypted in browser extension storage and in the Drive file, protected by your OS account and your Google account respectively.

Private by default

No account, no telemetry, no servers of ours. Uninstall the extension and its local data goes with it; delete the Drive file and the synced copy is gone.
